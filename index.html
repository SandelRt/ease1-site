Who made it

Designed by the people it's for.

"I was born with Cerebral Palsy, a physical disability, and I have been reviewing footwear since 2020. I have seen that Accessibility and Ease of Entry are often overlooked in footwear design. My studio pushes both of those elements to the forefront. All of our shoes are designed to be as easy to get on as possible."— Ryan Jans, Founder, Ease Footwear

The EASE 1 was designed by Sandel Concepcion of Lemniloop and built on Zellerfeld's Barefoot last — the first purpose-built easy-on sneaker on their platform.

Designed by the community it's for. That's the difference.

Before you buy

Questions, answered.

Will it actually fit? +

Yes. It's printed from your own foot scan and reengineered to your exact sizes. You can scan before you order with Zellerfeld's foot scanning partner, Volumental.

Are 3D-printed shoes durable? +

ZellerFoam is engineered to be breathable, soft and durable. It's great for all-day wear. ZellerFoam is also reusable.

Is it really that easy to put on? +

That's the whole point. A semi-collapsible heel lets you step in and go. No hands or bending needed.

What does "barefoot" mean? +

A zero millimeter heel-to-toe drop and minimal underfoot cushioning. It helps your foot move naturally, promoting foot health. Also, the Barefoot last makes the EASE 1 very stable.

How do I get my size right? +

Zellerfeld walks you through a quick phone scan of your feet. See the guide →

Why $189? +

It's made to fit, printed on demand, with free delivery — no warehouse markup and no waste. Comparable barefoot and adaptive sneakers sell off-the-shelf in a similar range, without the custom fit.
